Welcome to my website!

I am a 5th year PhD candidate at the University of Chicago Department of Astronomy and Astrophysics.

My research focuses on using high-resolution spectroscopy to study the stars in the Milky Way, particularly the dwarf galaxies in and around it! I am a part of the Near Field Cosmology group at UChicago, and I work primarily with Dr. Alex Ji. I am also a member of SDSS-V.
